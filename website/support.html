Support & changelog

Every version, and where to reach us.

A running history of what changed in each Continuum release, plus the fastest ways to get help, leave a review, or read the source.

Version history

Newest first. Continuum is updated often, with most releases focused on broader capture and cleaner hand-offs.

  1. 1.3.3 Latest
    Jul 1, 2026

    Stability and maintenance.

  2. 1.3.2 Improved
    Jun 29, 2026
    • Refined the interface to a smaller, more minimal panel with a cleaner, lighter layout.
  3. 1.3.1 Fix
    Jun 28, 2026
    • Fixed the extension not showing on an empty “new chat” page.
  4. 1.3.0 New
    Jun 24, 2026
    • Refined export formatting. PDF and Markdown histories now use a cleaner, more readable layout, consistent across AI-compressed and full exports.
    • Broader capture coverage. Captures more of each conversation, reducing the chance of missing content.
    • New download option. Export a conversation as PDF or Markdown with a single click.
  5. 1.2.0 Improved
    Jun 22, 2026
    • Smarter AI compression. Instead of just cutting old messages, it turns the whole chat into a structured summary (completed work, current state, in progress, next steps, constraints, critical context, discarded attempts) while preserving your decisions, code, files, and images, plus a custom resume message.
    • Full Gemini capture. Loads the entire conversation before capturing, so long, virtualized chats are saved in full with no manual scrolling.
    • Search across your saved AI chats.
  1. 1.1.1Fix
    Jun 20, 2026
    • Fixed the extension not working or showing on logged-out AI chats.

Get in touch

Hit a bug, have a question, or want to suggest a feature? We’d love to hear from you. The fastest way to reach us is by email, and we usually reply within 24 hours.
